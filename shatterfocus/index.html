ShatterFocus Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

ShatterFocus ("the extension") does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

What data is stored, and where

Your blocked website list, wait-timer settings, and redirect preference are stored locally on your own device using Chrome's built-in local storage API (chrome.storage.local). This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server, including any server operated by the developer.

Third parties

ShatterFocus does not use analytics, tracking, or any third-party service. No data is shared with anyone.

Permissions

The extension requests access to all websites so it can check whether the current page matches a site on your personal blocklist. This check happens entirely on your device — no browsing data is read, logged, or transmitted.
